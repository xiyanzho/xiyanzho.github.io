about

Such grounding draws me to machine learning: I like problems where careful mathematics and empirical systems meet.

research

I am broadly interested in reinforcement learning and in applying learning-based methods to biomechanical problems. I enjoy work that connects mathematical foundations in optimization and probability with empirical systems.

Quantifying Thigh Muscle Variability via Automated CT Segmentation
Undergraduate Research Assistant · advised by Prof. Jingwen Hu (UMTRI) · IOE SURE Program · May 2026 – Present

Building the lower-extremity muscle segmentation component of UMTRI's HERMES human body model. Established an automated CT preprocessing pipeline and a hand-labeled 3D ground-truth dataset (45 clinical scans) to fine tune the nnU-Net architecture behind TotalSegmentator so it can resolve individual thigh muscles. Outputs preserve the muscle orientation and cross-sectional area needed for active crash modeling.

poster
Motion Planning via Contrastive Reinforcement Learning
Research Assistant · advised by Kellen Kanarios · Jan 2026 – Present

Studying goal-conditioned contrastive RL with a Gumbel-Softmax actor for discrete action spaces, extending the JaxGCRL framework by replacing continuous distributions with categorical logits. Investigating within-episode distribution shift as a failure mode of the contrastive objective — complementary to the state–goal mapping limitations raised by Matthews et al., Goal-Conditioned Agents that Learn Everything All at Once. Implementing double Q-learning with a planned hierarchical extension, validated on Navix grid-world environments.

projects

Replicating and Extending SimGRL: Robustness in Visual RL under Dynamic Scene Perturbations

Replicated the SimGRL visual-RL framework (PyTorch, DMControl) and designed four data-augmentation extensions — Irregular-SRO, Multi-Source SRO, Hybrid-SRO, and Mask-Guided — to mitigate observational overfitting. Ran ablation studies on the Great Lakes HPC cluster; the Hybrid-SRO variant significantly reduced performance variance, giving a more stable training protocol for high-dimensional visual control.

Predicting ICU Mortality

Built a logistic-regression model for 30-day ICU mortality using engineered features such as instability scores, crossover counts, and time-windowed statistics. Applied KNN imputation, feature selection, and threshold tuning to balance AUROC and F1 for clinical relevance.
